    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in Pinellas Park, FL

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in Pinellas Park

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. It is driven by DHT, a hormone made from testosterone that slowly shrinks scalp follicles in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, which is why it follows a predictable pattern at the temples and crown. The two main medicines are finasteride, which cuts DHT, and minoxidil, which lengthens the growth phase. Combined use tends to work better than one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Female pattern hair loss is the most common cause, but thyroid problems, low iron, a recent pregnancy, crash dieting and major stress can all cause shedding that looks similar. Topical minoxidil is the approved starting point. Off-label oral options, such as spironolactone, are added when appropriate.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in Pinellas Park

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Minoxidil, FDA-approved as a topical, keeps follicles in the growth phase longer so strands come in thicker. The pairing is the standard. A compounded combination spray is another route, though it is not FDA-approved.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. Approved for prostate enlargement, it is used off-label for hair, usually after finasteride falls short. It lingers. Because it stays in the body for months, providers screen more carefully. Minoxidil is added for growth.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Pinellas Park

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. In this blend, minoxidil drives regrowth and GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, has early research suggesting it may help the scalp environment. It is compounded. The blend has no FDA approval. It suits women, and men avoiding finasteride.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. Expect questions about family plans, libido concerns, mood history and pills versus topicals. Most men begin with the finasteride and minoxidil pairing. Minoxidil is the usual first step for women.

    What to Expect Over Time in Pinellas Park

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. Visible change takes three to six months, and full results take about a year, so patience is part of the plan. Some people shed more in weeks two through eight of minoxidil, which usually means follicles are cycling into a new growth phase. Results last only as long as treatment does.
